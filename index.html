The Goblin Kingdom

An endless raid beneath the Goblin Fort. Beat Kragmaw the Warchief to open the gate, then clear floor after floor of the King's goblins for gold. Every run starts on floor 1.

Past floor 50 the five come back stronger, lap after lap.

  • Kragmaw the Warchief. Strike the War Gong in the Fort's War Hall to fight him and his warband.
  • New boss gear. Every new boss drops its own armor set, shield and weapon, with set perks like bombs, hexes, gores and crits.
  • New goblins. Warband Raiders and Brutes, Kingdom Guards, Berserkers, Champions and Hex Shades.
  • Deepest Floor. A Kingdom leaderboard in the Journal, plus badges for floors 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100.

Ore

32 ores, from Stone to Elderbloom

Everything you mine sells for gold, feeds your pets, or fortifies your gear at Grimbold's Forge, where better ore gives a bigger bonus.

Reading the core sample

Each bar is one ore's sell value per piece, all drawn on the same scale, coloured by rarity. Elderbloom, the deepest, sells for 3,400 gold. Stone sells for 1.

Pickaxes

Every pickaxe swings differently

21 pickaxes, each with its own swing. The top ones change who you are: Dawn and Dusk give you wings and a halo, Voidbreaker rips open rifts, and Worldroot rides a pillar of roots.

Scroll sideways for the full ladder. Prices are in gold at Whiskers' Wares or Old Tobias's forge.

Bosses

Eighteen bosses, and they hit hard

Raid bosses have phases, telegraphed attacks and summons. Most drop their own armor set, shield and weapon at 15%. Health scales up with your party.

Keep growing

Systems for the long haul

Enchanting

39 weapon and 31 pickaxe enchants, Common up to Mythic, at the Enchanter's Altar.

Fortifying

Ten ore levels on every pickaxe, weapon, shield and armor piece at Grimbold's Forge.

Armor sets

Mix helmets, chestplates and greaves. A full set gives bonus health and a perk.

Collection Book

Fill in every ore, creature, piece of gear and pet for gold and permanent perks.

Badges & dailies

72 badges, daily quests, login streaks, rebirths and global leaderboards.

Questlines

Five storylines: Eclipse, Void, Sky, Brood and Root. Each one opens a new part of the world.
